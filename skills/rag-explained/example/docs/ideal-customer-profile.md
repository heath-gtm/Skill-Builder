# Ideal customer profile

## Who we win with
Owner-run home services companies with 8 to 60 technicians, at least two years in business, and a dispatcher who still schedules from a whiteboard or a spreadsheet.

## The signal that matters most
A company that just opened a second location. Two locations is where whiteboard dispatch breaks, and the owner feels it within weeks.

## Who we walk away from
Companies under 3 technicians, because the software costs more than the time it saves them. National franchises, because the franchisor picks the software, not the local owner.
