# Competitor notes

## Brightline
Brightline is the most common competitor in deals with more than 40 technicians. It has stronger accounting integrations. It has no route optimization, and its customers often add a second tool for it.

## Toolbox Pro
Toolbox Pro is cheaper on paper and common in deals under 10 technicians. Its price rises when a company adds office staff, because it charges for every seat. Ask the prospect to count their office staff before comparing prices.
