## Question
When do we lose to Brightline and what is our advantage?

## Sources
[competitor-notes#1] Brightline
Brightline is the most common competitor in deals with more than 40 technicians. It has stronger accounting integrations. It has no route optimization, and its customers often add a second tool for it.

[discount-policy#2] What we never discount
Implementation for the Scale plan is never discounted, because a rushed rollout is the top reason Scale customers churn in their first year.

[ideal-customer-profile#1] Who we win with
Owner-run home services companies with 8 to 60 technicians, at least two years in business, and a dispatcher who still schedules from a whiteboard or a spreadsheet.

[ideal-customer-profile#3] Who we walk away from
Companies under 3 technicians, because the software costs more than the time it saves them. National franchises, because the franchisor picks the software, not the local owner.

## Answer
The notes do not say when we lose to Brightline. They say Brightline is the most common competitor in deals with more than 40 technicians [competitor-notes#1]. Its edge is stronger accounting integrations [competitor-notes#1]. Its weakness is that it has no route optimization, so its customers often add a second tool for it [competitor-notes#1]. The notes do not explicitly say that we offer route optimization [competitor-notes#1].
