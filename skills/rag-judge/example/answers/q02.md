## Question
Which plan includes route optimization, and how many technicians is it for?

## Sources
[pricing-and-packaging#2] Plans
There are three plans. Starter is for shops with up to 5 technicians and includes scheduling, a customer portal, and text reminders. Growth is for 6 to 40 technicians and adds route optimization and a dispatch board. Scale is for more than 40 technicians and adds multi-location reporting and an API.

[competitor-notes#1] Brightline
Brightline is the most common competitor in deals with more than 40 technicians. It has stronger accounting integrations. It has no route optimization, and its customers often add a second tool for it.

[pricing-and-packaging#4] Implementation
Starter is self-serve. Growth includes a 30-day guided setup. Scale includes a named implementation manager for the first 90 days.

[pricing-and-packaging#3] How we charge
Every plan is priced per technician per month and billed annually. Monthly billing is available on Starter only. Office staff seats are free on every plan, because charging for them made customers share logins.

## Answer
The Growth plan adds route optimization, along with a dispatch board, and it is for shops with 6 to 40 technicians [pricing-and-packaging#2]. The Scale plan, for more than 40 technicians, is described as adding multi-location reporting and an API [pricing-and-packaging#2].
