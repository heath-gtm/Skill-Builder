## Question
Why do we never discount Scale implementation?

## Sources
[discount-policy#2] What we never discount
Implementation for the Scale plan is never discounted, because a rushed rollout is the top reason Scale customers churn in their first year.

[pricing-and-packaging#4] Implementation
Starter is self-serve. Growth includes a 30-day guided setup. Scale includes a named implementation manager for the first 90 days.

[discount-policy#3] Multi-year deals
A two-year term earns an extra 5% off the second year, on top of any approved discount. We do not offer terms longer than two years.

[pricing-and-packaging#2] Plans
There are three plans. Starter is for shops with up to 5 technicians and includes scheduling, a customer portal, and text reminders. Growth is for 6 to 40 technicians and adds route optimization and a dispatch board. Scale is for more than 40 technicians and adds multi-location reporting and an API.

## Answer
We never discount implementation for the Scale plan because a rushed rollout is the top reason Scale customers churn in their first year [discount-policy#2]. For context, Scale implementation includes a named implementation manager for the first 90 days [pricing-and-packaging#4].
