## Question
Do office staff seats cost extra?

## Sources
[pricing-and-packaging#3] How we charge
Every plan is priced per technician per month and billed annually. Monthly billing is available on Starter only. Office staff seats are free on every plan, because charging for them made customers share logins.

[competitor-notes#2] Toolbox Pro
Toolbox Pro is cheaper on paper and common in deals under 10 technicians. Its price rises when a company adds office staff, because it charges for every seat. Ask the prospect to count their office staff before comparing prices.

[discount-policy#3] Multi-year deals
A two-year term earns an extra 5% off the second year, on top of any approved discount. We do not offer terms longer than two years.

## Answer
No. Office staff seats are free on every plan, because charging for them made customers share logins [pricing-and-packaging#3]. Plans are priced per technician per month instead [pricing-and-packaging#3]. This differs from Toolbox Pro, which charges for every seat, so its price rises when a company adds office staff [competitor-notes#2].
