## Answer
The strongest buying signal is **a company that just opened a second location** [ideal-customer-profile#2]. At two locations, whiteboard dispatch breaks down, and the owner feels it within weeks [ideal-customer-profile#2]. That pain fits Fieldstone's offering, since it sells scheduling and dispatch software to home services companies in plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and pest control [pricing-and-packaging#1].
