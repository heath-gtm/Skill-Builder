## Question
What is the per-technician price of the Growth plan?

## Sources
[pricing-and-packaging#3] How we charge
Every plan is priced per technician per month and billed annually. Monthly billing is available on Starter only. Office staff seats are free on every plan, because charging for them made customers share logins.

[objection-handling#2] "It's too expensive"
Ask what one missed appointment costs them, including the drive time. Most owners say between 150 and 300 dollars. Ten missed appointments a month usually covers the Growth plan.

[objection-handling#3] "My techs won't use an app"
The technician app has three buttons: on my way, started, done. Offer a two-week pilot with their least technical technician.

[discount-policy#1] Who can approve what
A rep can offer up to 10% off the annual price on their own. Between 10% and 20% needs the sales manager. Anything above 20% needs the head of sales, and comes with a written reason in the deal record.
