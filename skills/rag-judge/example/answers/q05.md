## Question
How should a rep handle a prospect who says their technicians won't use an app?

## Sources
[objection-handling#3] "My techs won't use an app"
The technician app has three buttons: on my way, started, done. Offer a two-week pilot with their least technical technician.

[objection-handling#1] "We already use spreadsheets"
Ask how they handle a technician calling in sick at 7am. The answer is usually a dispatcher making a dozen calls. Show the dispatch board reassigning the day in one screen.

[competitor-notes#2] Toolbox Pro
Toolbox Pro is cheaper on paper and common in deals under 10 technicians. Its price rises when a company adds office staff, because it charges for every seat. Ask the prospect to count their office staff before comparing prices.

[discount-policy#1] Who can approve what
A rep can offer up to 10% off the annual price on their own. Between 10% and 20% needs the sales manager. Anything above 20% needs the head of sales, and comes with a written reason in the deal record.

## Answer
Point out that the technician app is simple: it has just three buttons, "on my way," "started," and "done" [objection-handling#3]. Then offer a two-week pilot with the prospect's least technical technician [objection-handling#3].
