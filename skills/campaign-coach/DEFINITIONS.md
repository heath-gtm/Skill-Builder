# Campaign definitions

Fill this in once. Keep it wherever your sending agent reads its instructions, usually a `CLAUDE.md`, a system prompt, or a file the sequence template points at.

Why this exists: a readout is only as good as its terms. "Positive reply" means something different at every company, and an agent cannot apply a definition nobody wrote down. Every argument about whether a campaign worked is really an argument about one of the lines below.

Rules for filling it in:

- Write what is true today, not what you wish were true. You can tighten it after the first readout.
- Every definition has to be checkable by someone who was not there. If two people could read a reply and disagree, the definition is not finished.
- Leave a line blank rather than guessing. A blank is honest and the readout will flag it. A guess quietly corrupts every comparison after it.
