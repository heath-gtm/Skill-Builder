# Who we say no to

Owner: [name]
Last decided: [date]

**This is the document almost nobody has, and it changes AI output more than the
other three combined.** An assistant given only an ideal customer profile will
qualify everything in, because nothing gives it grounds to refuse. This is what
lets it say no.

**Do not invent this one.** Fifteen minutes with your two best reps, asking what
makes them walk away, fills it. They already know. It has never been written down.

Start from these prompts if the room goes quiet:

- What kind of deal do we win and then lose at renewal?
- What did our last five closed lost deals have in common?
- What does a rep wish they had known on call one?
- Which customer are we least proud of, and what was true about them at the start?
- What do we say yes to because the logo is good, and regret?
