# Cut the Drag: put AI on the reps

## What this does
Sorts one workflow into the repeatable drag that AI should run and the judgment that stays human, then puts AI on the drag. The drag is the execution between the steps: enrich, route, log, follow up, assemble. On most lean teams the split is inverted, people grind the drag while AI does nothing.

## What you'll need
One workflow with its steps stacked (the output of Stack the Tech). No connectors required to plan it.

## The method
1. List the drag. Every repeatable execution task between the steps. Be specific: "pull the last three touches and summarize" is a task, "do research" is not.
2. Ask one question per task. Does it need human judgment, real context, or a relationship? If yes, it stays human. If it is repeatable execution, it is drag.
3. Put AI on the safe drag. Automate the repeatable tasks that are safe to hand off. Start with one, prove it, then the next.
4. Flag the not-yet. Where the drag is not safe to automate, say so out loud. Do not force it.
5. Watch the cleanup. A sloppy cut creates rework that eats the savings. If the automation makes more drag than it removes, it is not done.

## Failure points
- Automating a task that carried context you only notice once it is gone.
- Shipping a sloppy cut and paying for the cleanup.
- Calling it "replace the team" instead of "cut the drag." The goal is people doing the part only people can.
