## How to use this

Your AI should be connected to this folder, not holding uploaded copies of it.
Nobody downloads these. Nobody uploads them to a personal chat. Nobody keeps
their own copy. A copy is a photocopy, frozen the moment it was made, and
nothing will ever tell you it went stale.

## The monthly check

Once a month, ask the assistant: **who do we disqualify?**

If the answer is wrong or generic, something upstream changed and nobody told
these documents. That is the entire monitoring system. It takes thirty seconds.
